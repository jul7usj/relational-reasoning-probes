# Milestone 1 — first relational probe

**Status:** design phase. Not implemented.

**Goal:** test whether the relational structure of activations across
reasoning steps in a small open-source transformer is more predictive of
reasoning success than the activations' coordinate values.

**Model and benchmark to be selected.** Selection and justification will
be written to `notes/` once design begins.

**Pre-registration commitment:** the falsifiable prediction stated in the
top-level technical report (`report/v0_outline.md`, Section 1.1) must be
pre-registered in writing before any probe is trained, with no revision
of the prediction after results are known.
